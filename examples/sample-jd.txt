Senior Backend Engineer, Payments Platform

About Ledgerly
Ledgerly builds real-time payments infrastructure for 4,000+ merchants across North America. Our platform moves $2B in transactions every month.

What you'll do
- Design, build and operate high-throughput microservices in Go that process payment transactions with strict correctness guarantees
- Build event-driven pipelines with Apache Kafka for settlement, reconciliation and fraud signals
- Own services end-to-end on AWS (EKS, RDS, SQS) including on-call, observability and incident response
- Improve reliability and latency of our payment APIs; define SLOs and drive them with Prometheus and Grafana
- Partner with product, risk and compliance teams to ship features for merchants
- Mentor engineers and lead technical design reviews

Requirements
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience building backend systems
- Strong proficiency in Go (Golang) or Java, and willingness to work primarily in Go
- Deep experience with distributed systems, concurrency and designing RESTful APIs and gRPC services
- Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL and Redis at scale
- Experience with Kafka or similar event streaming platforms
- Experience with Docker, Kubernetes and CI/CD pipelines
- Solid understanding of AWS
- Excellent communication skills and cross-functional collaboration

Nice to have
- Experience in fintech, payments, or ledger systems
- Terraform / Infrastructure as Code
- Familiarity with PCI DSS compliance
- OpenTelemetry distributed tracing

Benefits
Competitive salary, equity, remote-friendly, 401(k) match, health insurance.
